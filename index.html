Who is Dr. Ania Jastreboff?
Dr. Ania Jastreboff, MD, PhD

Dr. Ania Jastreboff is an Associate Professor of Medicine (Endocrinology & Metabolism) and Psychiatry at Yale School of Medicine, and Director of the Yale Metabolic Health & Weight Loss Program. A dual MD-PhD physician-scientist, she has dedicated her career to understanding the biological mechanisms behind weight gain and developing evidence-based approaches to sustainable weight loss.
